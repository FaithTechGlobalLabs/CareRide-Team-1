## Criteria 1: Problem
E.g. How clear is the project? How much research is behind validating this is an actual problem? How much research into existing solutions?

## Criteria 2: Potential
E.g. How many people does this problem (and thus, project) affect? What's the potential for the project to spread to other organizations and spheres?

## Criteria 3: Approach
E.g. Does the proposed solution adequately address the problem? Is the solution feasible? Is the solution efficient and effective? Competitive Research.

## Criteria 4: Deliverable
E.g. If an MVP, did the MVP fulfill the core functionality during the hackathon? If a prototype, did it adequately validate the assumptions of the project?

## Criteria 5: Excellence
E.g. How well did the project team do given their potential (e.g. professional experience and skillset, etc) and constraints (e.g. time frame, project owner's requirements, unexpected obstacles, team assigned, etc.)?

## Criteria 6: Product Plan
E.g. How well will it scale beyond the MVP?  How much consideration has been given to integration with the client’s technical stack?
